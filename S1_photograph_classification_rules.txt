S1 Photograph classification rules
==================================

The internal labels No damage and Slight damage are merged into the Intact class, while
moderate, severe and collapse reference grades are mapped to Damaged. Cases with
insufficient visual evidence were referred to engineers for more detailed inspection.

Agent 2 reports findings; code validates them and assigns each finding an accepted,
excluded or uncertain disposition. Its proposed_class does not determine the photograph
class. Apply the following rules in order to a valid finding.

First, an uncertain assessment gives an uncertain disposition. Otherwise, non_damage is
excluded; otherwise, unknown severity is uncertain. Apply the matrix below to the
remaining valid damage findings, whose severity is slight or severe.

Structural elements are wall, column, beam, opening_edge, building_corner and
wall_junction. The following matrix applies after the preceding checks.

Finding kind            Structural element  Floor or ground  Roof            Unknown
----------------------  ------------------  ---------------  --------------  ---------
crack or spalling       Accepted            Excluded         Uncertain       Uncertain
crushing or separation  Accepted            Uncertain        Uncertain       Uncertain
debris or collapse      Ownership rule      Ownership rule   Ownership rule  Uncertain
other                   Uncertain           Uncertain        Uncertain       Uncertain

Ownership rule: debris or collapse is accepted when belongs_to_building is true,
excluded when false, and uncertain when null. Cables, shadows, vegetation, mortar
joints, construction joints and surface patterns cannot be confirmed as damage; a reply
that assigns assessment = damage to one of these kinds is invalid.

Photograph class, by priority (the first matching condition applies)

1. At least one accepted finding has severity severe
   Photograph class: Severe damage

2. Otherwise, at least one accepted finding has severity slight
   Photograph class: Slight damage

3. Otherwise, no uncertain finding, clear_view = true and clean_surface_evidence = true
   Photograph class: No damage

4. Otherwise
   Photograph class: null

Excluded findings never set the class. Uncertain findings prevent No damage but do not
override an accepted Slight or Severe finding. A fine superficial finish crack without
structural involvement can therefore yield Slight damage and map to Intact. Positive
clean-surface evidence is required for No damage, rather than for every Intact
prediction.
